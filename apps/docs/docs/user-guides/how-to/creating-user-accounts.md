# Creating a HydroServer User Account

While you can navigate to a HydroServer instance and interact with any metadata and data that have been made public, creating a workspace or becoming a collaborator on a workspace requires creating a user account. HydroServer enables multiple options for user account creation that are controlled by the administrator of the HydroServer instance. The default option is simple authentication (an email as a username and a chosen password). Administrators can also enable sign-in with third-party accounts such as Google, ORCID, or HydroShare.

## Signing Up

When you go to a HydroServer instance, you can click the 'Sign up' button in the top right corner, or click 'Log in' and follow the sign-up link on the **Sign in to HydroServer** page.

<img src="/data-management-app/login-page.png" alt="Sign in to HydroServer page" class="img-white-bg">

If the HydroServer administrator has enabled third-party accounts, their buttons are shown first on the sign-in page, alongside 'Continue with email', and under 'Other sign-up options' on the sign-up page. Choose a provider to create your account with it, or sign up with email.

To sign up with email, enter your email address and choose a password, and fill out your user information on the **Sign up** form. If you are affiliated with an organization, check 'Affiliated with an organization' and fill out the organization information as well. We recommend completing your user information, because it identifies you to the collaborators on your workspaces. Then click the 'Sign up' button.

<img src="/data-management-app/account-page.png" alt="Sign up form" class="img-white-bg">

As a last step, HydroServer sends a verification code to your email address. Enter that code on the **Check your inbox** page and click the 'Verify email' button to finish.

## Managing Your Account

To view your account, sign in, click the account icon in the top right corner, and select **Account**. Your profile page shows your user and organization information and links to manage your account:

- **Edit**: update your name, contact information, user type, and organization affiliation, then click 'Save changes'.
- **Change password**: set a new password. If you have forgotten your password, use the 'Forgot Password?' link to receive a password reset email.
- **Sign out**: sign out of HydroServer.
- **Delete account**: permanently delete your account. Deleting your account also deletes any workspaces you own and all data within them, and the page lists those workspaces before you confirm. To keep a workspace, transfer its ownership to another user first from the workspace's **Ownership** tab on the [Manage workspaces](/user-guides/how-to/managing-workspaces.md) page.
